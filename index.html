SAFETY / 03

Keep secrets out of the scanner.

Do not enter passwords, email addresses, API keys, bearer tokens, or live credentials. CookieScope does not log in, log out, crawl a site, or send data to a server.

Use only cookies from systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. The “Show values” option is for local testing visibility only and keeps values in your browser.
